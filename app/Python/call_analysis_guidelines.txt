Call Analysis – AI-Based Verification Call Analysis
1. Objective
The Call Analysis feature will use AI to analyze recorded Verification Calls between the Verification Executive and Service Provider (SP).
The primary purpose is to verify whether the conversation is:
Relevant to the Verification Process
Professional and appropriate
Free from abusive/bad language
Free from unrelated conversations
Properly conducted by the Verification Executive
Of acceptable communication quality
The AI should analyze the complete conversation and provide a Summary, Alerts, Highlighted Conversations, and Call Rating.

2. Call Analysis – Main Sections
The Call Analysis page should contain the following sections:
A. Call Summary
B. Conversation Analysis
C. Alerts
D. Call Rating
E. Call Quality
F. Communication Quality

3. Call Summary
The Summary is the main section of the Call Analysis.
AI should analyze the complete conversation and generate a concise summary of what was discussed during the call.
The summary should include:
Main purpose of the call
Verification-related discussion
Important information provided by the SP
Questions asked by the Verification Executive
Responses provided by the SP
Verification status/details discussed
Any issues identified during the call
Example
Summary:
The Verification Executive contacted the SP to complete the verification process. The executive verified the SP's basic details, discussed the required documents, and explained the verification process.
If unrelated conversation is detected, AI should mention it in the summary.

4. Unrelated Conversation Detection
AI should identify conversations that are not related to the Verification Process.
Examples:
Personal conversations
Movies
Songs
Politics
Sports
Personal relationships
Unnecessary discussions
Other unrelated topics
Example
Conversation:
Executive: Please confirm your name and address.
SP: My name is Rahul and I live in Hyderabad.
Executive: Okay. Which cricket team do you support?
The cricket-related conversation should be identified as Unrelated Conversation.
Highlight
The unrelated sentence should be highlighted in the Call Analysis.
Example:
Executive: Please confirm your address.
SP: I live in Hyderabad.
Executive: Which cricket team do you support? ⚠️
The highlighted sentence should be clearly visible to the Admin.

5. Bad / Abusive Language Detection
AI should detect if the SP or Verification Executive uses:
Bad words
Abusive language
Offensive language
Threatening language
Disrespectful communication
Inappropriate language
The identified sentence should be highlighted.
Example
Executive: Please upload your required document.
SP: [Offensive/abusive statement] ⚠️
The Admin should be able to identify:
Who used the language
Exact conversation timestamp
Category of violation
Highlighted sentence

6. Verification Process Relevance
AI should determine whether the call is actually being used for the intended Verification Process.
The AI should check whether the conversation contains verification-related topics such as:
SP identity verification
Personal/basic details
Address verification
Documents
Bank details, where applicable
Verification requirements
Service Provider information
Verification instructions
Verification status
Other approved verification questions
If the majority of the call is unrelated to verification, the system should generate an alert.
Alert Example
⚠️ Verification Relevance Alert
A significant portion of this call appears to be unrelated to the Service Provider verification process.

7. Song / Audio Upload Detection
The Verification Executive must upload the actual Verification Call recording.
AI should detect if the uploaded audio contains:
Songs
Music
Unrelated audio
Blank/empty audio
Excessive background noise
Audio that does not contain a verification conversation
Alert Example
🔴 Alert: Unrelated Audio Detected
The uploaded recording contains music/song content that is unrelated to the verification process.
The Admin should be able to review the recording and timestamp where the issue was detected.

8. Call Rating
The system should automatically generate a Call Rating based on the overall quality of the verification call.
The rating should consider:
A. Verification Relevance
Was the conversation related to the verification process?
B. Communication Quality
Was the communication clear and professional?
C. Professional Behaviour
Did the executive communicate appropriately?
D. Unrelated Conversation
Was unnecessary conversation detected?
E. Bad / Abusive Language
Was inappropriate language detected?
F. Verification Completion
Was the required verification process properly completed?

9. Call Rating Display
The Call Rating should be displayed prominently on the Call Analysis page.
Example:
Call Rating: 8.5 / 10
The rating should use a visual indicator.
Normal Call
Call Rating: 9.2 / 10
Issue Detected
Call Rating: 5.4 / 10 🔴
If the AI identifies serious issues such as abusive language, significant unrelated conversation, songs, or failure to conduct verification properly, the Call Rating should be displayed in Red.

11. Alerts
The system should generate alerts whenever a significant issue is detected.
Alert Types
🔴 Critical Alert
Abusive language
Threatening language
Fake/incorrect recording
Song/music uploaded instead of call
Call does not contain verification conversation
🟠 Warning
Excessive unrelated conversation
Poor communication
Excessive background noise
Verification process partially completed
🟢 Good
Verification process completed
Professional communication
Relevant conversation
No major issues detected

12. Conversation Highlighting
The Call Analysis should highlight specific sentences identified by AI.
Each highlighted conversation should show:
Speaker: Verification Executive / SP
Timestamp: 05:42
Category: Unrelated Conversation
Conversation: Identified sentence
Severity: Warning
Example
🔴 SP – 05:42 – Abusive Language
"[Detected inappropriate sentence]"
🟠 Executive – 07:15 – Unrelated Conversation
"[Detected unrelated sentence]"

Add Admin remarks

16. Important Business Rule
The AI Summary should be the primary section of Call Analysis.
The summary must not only provide a general overview. It should also identify important issues detected during the conversation.
If an unrelated, abusive, inappropriate, or verification-irrelevant sentence is detected:
AI should identify the sentence.
The sentence should be highlighted.
The category should be displayed.
Timestamp should be displayed.
An alert should be generated.
The Call Rating should be reduced based on severity.
If the issue is serious, the Call Rating should be displayed in Red.
