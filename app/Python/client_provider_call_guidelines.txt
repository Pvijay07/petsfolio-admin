AI Integration – Call Summary & Service Conversation Analysis (Client & Provider Calls)
Official Knowledge Base & Conversation Auditing Rules

1. Document Purpose
The purpose of this document is to define the comprehensive requirements for AI-based Call Summary, Conversation Analysis, and Policy Compliance in the Petsfolio platform.
Petsfolio has introduced Mobile-to-Mobile Calling between the Client/Pet Parent and Service Provider (SP). Calls are automatically recorded and analyzed.
The AI system analyzes recorded conversations and generates a structured Call Summary.
The primary objectives are:
- Ensure conversations between Clients and Service Providers are relevant to the booked Petsfolio service (Walking, Grooming, Training, Sitting).
- Accurately understand conversation flow using booking details, prior context, and speaker intent.
- Prevent false-positive highlighting of normal greetings, confirmations, pet routine discussions, and legitimate payment/charge questions.
- Detect genuine policy violations: Off-platform direct payments, personal contact sharing, Petsfolio platform bypass, abusive speech, threatening behavior, and unauthorized services.
- Highlight ONLY the specific sentences that warrant attention, rather than highlighting entire calls.

3. AI Analysis Objectives & Core Questions
Every completed call must answer three core questions:
1. What was discussed? (Concise summary of key service activities and discussions)
2. Was the conversation related to the booked Petsfolio service? (Service Relevance Score & Percentage)
3. Was there anything that requires Admin attention? (Policy violations, bypass attempts, complaints, refund requests, abusive language)

The AI evaluates:
- What service was booked versus discussed.
- Whether important service information was properly exchanged.
- Whether unrelated conversation occurred and whether it was minor banter or sustained unrelated discussion.
- Whether inappropriate, abusive, or threatening language was used.
- Whether service complaints or refund requests were made.
- Whether the SP made unauthorized commitments or the Client requested services outside the booking.
- Whether either party attempted to bypass Petsfolio, share personal contact details, or avoid platform/GST charges.

4. Supported Services & Service-Specific Conversation Analysis
The AI understands the four major Petsfolio service categories and normal conversations within each:

4.1 Walking Analysis
Recognizes discussions regarding:
- Walk date, walk time, walk duration
- Walk location, pickup/drop location
- Pet name, breed, age, activity level
- Pet walking requirements, leash requirements, harness
- Pet safety, street temperament, nervousness
- Toilet/poop requirements, paw cleaning, combing
- Number of walks scheduled, walk completion
- Delayed walk, missed walk, walker arrival
- Walk rescheduling, walk cancellation, walk-related complaints
Example:
Client: "Can you come tomorrow at 7 AM for Bruno's walk?"
SP: "Yes, I will come at 7 AM."
AI Classification: ✅ Service Related – Walking | Topic: Walk Timing | Highlight: No | Issue: None

4.2 Grooming Conversation Analysis
Recognizes discussions regarding:
- Grooming appointment date and time
- Grooming location (home grooming vs facility)
- Pet preparation, bath, shampoo, water temperature
- Hair trimming, styling, de-shedding
- Anti-tick treatment, medicated bath
- Nail trimming, ear cleaning, paw hygiene
- Pet behavior during grooming, grooming duration
- Grooming completion, grooming rescheduling, cancellation, complaints
Example:
Client: "I booked grooming for Sunday. Will you provide hair trimming?"
SP: "Yes, hair trimming is included in the selected grooming package."
AI Classification: ✅ Service Related – Grooming | Topic: Package Inclusions | Highlight: No | Issue: None

4.3 Training Conversation Analysis
Recognizes discussions regarding:
- Training schedule, session numbers, remaining sessions
- Puppy training, intermediate training, advanced training
- Behavior training, aggression management, leash pulling
- Commands (sit, stay, come, heel, down, leave it)
- Obedience, training progress, daily practice requirements
- Training duration, location, trainer arrival
- Training rescheduling, session cancellation, feedback, complaints
Example:
Client: "My dog is not following basic commands. Can you work on obedience training?"
Trainer: "Yes, we can focus on basic obedience and commands during the sessions."
AI Classification: ✅ Service Related – Training | Topic: Training Plan | Highlight: No | Issue: None

4.4 Sitting Conversation Analysis
Recognizes discussions regarding:
- Sitting booking dates, drop-off / pick-up timings
- Sitting duration, day care, overnight sitting, hourly sitting
- Pet feeding routine, pet food brand, feeding timings, treats
- Pet medication instructions, dosage, vet contact
- Sleeping arrangements, bedding, comfort items
- Pet routine, daily exercise, walks during sitting
- Pet behavior with other pets or family members
- Sitting completion, cancellation, rescheduling, complaints
Example:
Client: "My dog needs sitting from Saturday morning until Sunday morning."
SP: "Okay, I will provide overnight sitting as per the booking."
AI Classification: ✅ Service Related – Sitting | Topic: Sitting Schedule | Highlight: No | Issue: None

5. AI Contextual Understanding: Booking Info + Call Context + Conversation Flow
The AI must not rely on isolated keywords. It evaluates statements using three pillars:
A. Booking Information: Pre-existing service type, schedule, and customer profile.
B. Call Context: What was discussed in preceding and following sentences.
C. Conversation Context: The overall intent, tone, and outcome of the exchange.

Contextual Example:
"Is 7 okay for tomorrow?"
- Without context: Ambiguous.
- With preceding sentence: "Tomorrow's walk is scheduled for the morning."
- AI Classification: Service: Walking | Topic: Timing | Classification: Service Related | Highlight: No

6. False Highlight Prevention & Over-Highlighting Rules (Do NOT Over-Highlight)
The primary rule of conversation auditing is: DO NOT OVER-HIGHLIGHT.
Normal service conversation must appear normally in the summary without warning icons or highlight boxes.
A summary with 5 accurate highlights is far superior to a summary with 20 false highlights.

6.1 Normal Greetings (Highlight: No | Alert: No)
The following greetings are standard courtesy and must NEVER be highlighted:
- Hello, Hi, Hey
- Good morning, Good afternoon, Good evening
- How are you? How are you doing?
- Thank you, Thanks a lot
- Okay, Sure, Fine, Alright, Noted
- Bye, Take care, See you
Example:
Client: "Hi, good morning."
SP: "Good morning."
AI Classification: Normal greeting | Highlight: No | Issue: None

6.2 Normal Confirmation Statements (Highlight: No | Alert: No)
Statements confirming service execution must NEVER be flagged:
- "Okay, I will come at 7 AM."
- "Sure, I will be there."
- "Yes, I can provide the service."
- "Okay, I understood."
- "Sure, I will take care of Bruno."
- "Yes, tomorrow is fine."
- "Okay, noted."
AI Classification: Service Related / Normal Confirmation | Highlight: No | Issue: None

6.3 Normal Service Questions (Highlight: No | Alert: No)
Questions directly clarifying the booked service are normal communication:
- Walking: "What time should I come?", "Where should I pick up the dog?", "Does Bruno need paw cleaning?", "Should I bring a leash?"
- Grooming: "Is hair trimming included?", "What time should I arrive?", "Should the pet be bathed before?", "Is anti-tick treatment included?"
- Training: "What behavior should we focus on?", "What time is the session?", "Can we work on basic commands?"
- Sitting: "What time does feeding happen?", "How long is the sitting?", "Where should the pet sleep?"
AI Classification: Normal Service Inquiry | Highlight: No | Issue: None

6.4 Normal Pet-Related Information (Highlight: No | Alert: No)
Pet health, routine, temperament, or emotional state shared to help the SP provide better care is essential service context, NOT a complaint or problematic statement:
- "Bruno is a little nervous."
- "He usually takes some time to adjust to new people."
- "He likes walking in the morning."
- "She does not like strangers at first."
- "He usually eats at 8 AM."
- "My dog is very active."
- "He may bark initially."
- "She likes playing with balls."
- "Bruno is not feeling comfortable today."
Context Rule: In "Bruno is not feeling comfortable today", SP responds: "Okay, I will handle him carefully." This is vital pet care coordination.
Wrong AI Result: 🔴 Problematic Statement / Complaint (INCORRECT)
Correct AI Result: 🟢 Service/Pet Information | Category: Pet Temperament | Highlight: No | Issue: None

6.5 Short Personal Conversation (Level 2 Unrelated | Low Severity | Alert: No)
Brief social pleasantries or polite non-service questions do NOT warrant an Admin Alert or a Critical rating:
Example:
Client: "Where are you from?"
SP: "I'm from Hyderabad."
AI Classification: Service Related: No | Category: Short Personal Conversation | Severity: Low | Policy Violation: No | Admin Alert: No | Overall Rating: Remains 🟢 Good

7. Unrelated Conversation Detection & Mixed Conversation Handling

7.1 Sustained Unrelated Topics
When conversation continues on unrelated topics for a significant duration, the AI classifies those specific sentences as Unrelated:
- Sports & Cricket: IPL, cricket match scores, stadiums, football, tournaments
- Movies & Entertainment: Cinema, actors, Netflix shows, songs, celebrity gossip
- Politics & Government: Elections, political parties, voting, politicians
- Personal Real Estate & Vehicles: Buying a car (Honda, Hyundai), bike, buying a flat, property deals
- Outside Work & Business: Software development, crypto, stock market, unrelated jobs
- Personal Relationships: Dating, family gossip, marital affairs

7.2 Segmenting Mixed Conversations
A call can contain both service-related and unrelated segments. The AI segments each portion independently and NEVER marks the entire call as unrelated or problematic:
Example Segment Progression:
- 00:00–02:00: Walking discussion → 🟢 Service Related (Normal)
- 02:01–03:00: Cricket discussion → 🟡 Unrelated Conversation (Informational)
- 03:01–04:30: Paw cleaning instructions → 🟢 Service Related (Normal)
- 04:31–05:00: SP arrived late complaint → 🟠 Complaint (Review Required)

8. Inappropriate Language vs. Complaint Distinction

8.1 Context-Aware Language Rule
The AI must never highlight a sentence simply because it contains a negative word such as "bad", "angry", or "terrible". It must evaluate speaker intent and target:
- Negative Sentiment / Complaint: "That service was really bad yesterday" or "I am very angry because the walker did not arrive." → Category: Complaint / Negative Sentiment. NOT abusive language.
- Actual Abusive Language: Obscene words, personal insults, derogatory slurs directed at the person ("idiot", "moron", "useless fool", "shut up", abusive Hindi/regional words). → Category: Inappropriate Language | Highlight: Yes | Risk: Critical.

8.2 Legitimate Complaints (Highlight: Yes | Severity: Level 3 Review Required)
Highlight when the conversation indicates dissatisfaction with service execution:
- SP arrived late (e.g. "The walker came 30 minutes late today")
- SP did not arrive / no-show
- Pet was handled roughly or injured
- Incomplete grooming or poor haircut
- Trainer missed scheduled sessions
- Unprofessional conduct or unattended pet
Classification: Category: Complaint | Priority: Medium | Highlight: Yes | Admin Alert: Review Recommended

8.3 Threatening Communication & Intimidation (Highlight: Yes | Severity: Level 4 Critical)
Direct threats of harm, intimidation, or aggressive escalation:
- Threats against Client or SP safety
- Threats of physical violence or harassment
- Aggressive threats ("I will destroy your business", "I will get you fired")
Classification: Category: Threatening Communication | Highlight: Yes 🔴 | Risk: Critical | Admin Alert: Yes

9. Cancellation & Rescheduling Handling

9.1 Cancellation as a Topic (Service-Related Topic | Alert: No)
A cancellation discussion is a normal operational request, NOT a complaint, policy violation, or abusive statement:
Example:
Client: "I don't need tomorrow's walk. I want to cancel it."
SP: "Okay, I will update the schedule."
AI Classification: Service: Walking | Topic: Cancellation | Service Related: Yes | Complaint: No | Policy Violation: No | Admin Alert: No (processed via standard cancellation workflow)

9.2 Rescheduling as a Topic (Service-Related Topic | Alert: No)
Changing dates or times is normal customer coordination:
Example:
Client: "Can we move today's grooming appointment to tomorrow at 4 PM?"
SP: "Yes, 4 PM tomorrow is available."
AI Classification: Service: Grooming | Topic: Rescheduling | Service Related: Yes | Complaint: No | Policy Violation: No | Highlight: No

10. Refund Demands & Outside-Service Requests

10.1 Refund Requests (Highlight: Yes | Severity: Level 3 Review Required)
Refund discussions require Admin visibility for billing verification:
Example:
Client: "I want my money back because the service was not completed."
AI Classification: 🚨 Refund Request Detected | Category: Refund Request | Admin Review: Required | Highlight: Yes 🟠

10.2 Additional / Outside-Service Requests (Highlight: Yes | Severity: Level 3 Review Required)
When a Client asks the SP to perform tasks outside the booked service package:
Example:
Booked Service: Walking
Client: "I booked walking, but can you also groom my dog and give him a bath?"
AI Classification: ⚠️ Additional Service Request | Booked Service: Walking | Requested Service: Grooming | Highlight: Yes | Admin Review: Based on policy

11. Contact Sharing Detection & Policies

11.1 Mobile Number Discussion
The AI distinguishes between incidental mentions of phone numbers and intentional attempts to exchange personal contact details:
- Normal Reference: "My battery died so I couldn't check my phone" → Normal | Highlight: No
- Client Requesting Number: "Can you give me your personal mobile number?" → Category: Contact Sharing | Highlight: Yes 🔴 | Admin Alert: Yes
- SP Sharing Number: "Save my personal number, call me directly anytime." → Category: Contact Sharing / Platform Bypass | Highlight: Yes 🔴 | Admin Alert: Yes

11.2 WhatsApp & Personal Messaging Transition
Attempts to shift communication away from Petsfolio's monitored calling system:
Example:
Client: "Can I WhatsApp you directly?"
SP: "Yes, message me on my personal WhatsApp number."
AI Classification: Category: Contact Sharing / Off-Platform Communication | Highlight: Yes 🔴 | Admin Alert: Yes

12. Petsfolio Platform Bypass & Direct Booking Detection

12.1 Direct Booking Outside Petsfolio
Directly bypassing Petsfolio for upcoming or recurring bookings is a critical violation:
Example:
Client: "Next time, I will contact you directly instead of booking through Petsfolio."
SP: "Yes, you can contact me directly."
AI Classification: Category: Petsfolio Bypass / Platform Bypass | Highlight: Yes 🚨 | Risk Level: Critical | Admin Alert: Yes

12.2 Direct Off-Platform Payment
Attempts to pay cash, personal UPI (GPay, PhonePe), or bank transfer directly to the SP:
Example:
Client: "Can I pay you directly instead of paying through the Petsfolio app?"
SP: "Yes, you can send it to my personal GPay."
AI Classification: Category: Off-Platform Payment | Highlight: Yes 🚨 | Risk Level: Critical | Admin Alert: Yes

12.3 SP Encouraging Bypass
When the SP initiates the platform circumvention:
Example:
SP: "Don't book through Petsfolio next time. Just call me directly and I will come."
AI Classification: Category: Platform Bypass (SP Initiated) | Highlight: Yes 🚨 | Risk Level: Critical | Admin Alert: Yes

12.4 Client Encouraging Bypass & SP Agreement
Client: "Next time I'll contact you directly. We don't need Petsfolio."
SP: "Okay, sounds good."
AI Classification: Category: Platform Bypass (Mutual Agreement) | Highlight: Yes 🚨 | Risk Level: Critical | Admin Alert: Yes

13. GST & Platform Charges: Context-Aware Detection
The terms "GST", "taxes", "platform charge", or "convenience fee" MUST NEVER trigger an automatic alert or violation by themselves. Context and intent determine the classification.

13.1 Normal GST Inquiries (Highlight: No | Violation: No | Alert: No)
Legitimate questions seeking clarification about invoice breakdown:
Example 1:
Client: "How much GST is included in my service amount?"
SP: "The applicable GST is included as shown on your Petsfolio booking receipt."
AI Classification: Category: Service/Payment Information | Highlight: No | Policy Violation: No | Admin Alert: No

Example 2:
Client: "Why is GST charged on pet services?"
SP: "Please contact Petsfolio Support for billing and GST clarification."
AI Classification: Category: Normal Payment Question | Highlight: No | Policy Violation: No | Admin Alert: No

13.2 Normal Platform Charge Questions (Highlight: No | Violation: No | Alert: No)
Example:
Client: "Why are platform charges added to my booking?"
SP: "Platform charges cover app support and verified provider guarantees."
AI Classification: Category: Normal Billing Question | Highlight: No | Policy Violation: No | Admin Alert: No

13.3 GST & Platform Charges Used as an Excuse to Bypass Petsfolio (Highlight: Yes | Severity: Level 4 Critical)
When GST or platform charges are cited specifically to justify off-platform direct transactions:
Example 1:
Client: "Petsfolio is charging GST and platform charges. Can I pay you directly without these extra charges?"
SP: "Yes, pay me directly and we can save the platform charges."
AI Classification: 🚨 Platform Bypass / Off-Platform Payment | GST Discussion: Yes | Platform Charges Discussion: Yes | Bypass Intent: Yes | Direct Payment: Yes | Risk Level: Critical | Highlight: Yes 🚨 | Admin Alert: Yes

Example 2:
SP: "If you book through Petsfolio, they will add GST and platform fees. Give me cash directly and it's cheaper."
AI Classification: 🚨 Platform Bypass (SP Initiated) / Fee Evasion | Risk Level: Critical | Highlight: Yes 🚨 | Admin Alert: Yes

14. Multi-Signal Combination Risk Detection
When multiple violation signals occur in the same call, the AI escalates the overall severity to Critical:
- Signal A: Mobile number sharing / request
- Signal B: WhatsApp / external messaging shift
- Signal C: Direct personal payment discussion (cash, personal UPI)
- Signal D: Future booking bypass discussion
- Signal E: Avoiding platform charges
- Signal F: Avoiding GST / platform fees

Combination Rule: If two or more signals (e.g., Signal A + Signal D, or Signal C + Signal F) appear in the same call, classify as Critical Policy Violation with immediate Admin Alert.

15. 4-Level Highlight Severity Taxonomy
To ensure clarity on the Admin dashboard, all statements are mapped to four discrete levels:

Level 1 – Normal (No Highlight)
- Service scheduling and timing
- Normal confirmation statements
- Pet routine, habits, nervousness, comfort descriptions
- Package and service requirements
- Normal service inquiries and payment clarification questions

Level 2 – Informational / Unrelated (Optional Visual Identification | Low Severity | No Admin Alert)
- Short personal conversation and pleasantries ("Where are you from?", "How was your weekend?")
- Minor non-service topics that do not disrupt service planning

Level 3 – Review Required (Highlight Yes 🟠 | Priority: Medium | Admin Review: Recommended)
- Service quality complaints (lateness, rough handling, incomplete session)
- Refund requests and billing disputes
- Sustained unrelated conversation occupying a substantial portion of the call
- Additional service requests outside the booked package
- Negative customer or provider sentiment

Level 4 – Critical (Highlight Yes 🔴 | Risk: Critical | Admin Alert: YES)
- Off-platform direct payment discussion
- Personal contact sharing (mobile number, WhatsApp, email exchange)
- Petsfolio platform bypass proposals (by Client or SP)
- Fee evasion (using GST / platform charges to bypass)
- Abusive, profane, or insulting language
- Threatening, intimidating, or harassing communication
- Serious unresolved disputes

16. Highlighting Specificity: Sentence-Level Highlighting
The AI must highlight ONLY the specific sentence or segment that contains the issue.
DO NOT highlight the entire conversation or mark the complete transcript red because of a single offending statement.
Example:
00:10 SP: "Tomorrow's walk is at 7 AM." → (Normal, No highlight)
00:15 Client: "Yes, I will keep Bruno ready." → (Normal, No highlight)
00:20 Client: "Can I give you payment directly to save platform fees?" → 🔴 Highlight: Yes (Level 4: Off-Platform Payment)
00:25 SP: "Yes, send it to my GPay." → 🔴 Highlight: Yes (Level 4: Off-Platform Payment)
00:30 Client: "Also remember to clean his paws after the walk." → (Normal, No highlight)

17. AI Confidence & Uncertainty Handling
- High Confidence: Clear service coordination or clear policy violation → Classify directly.
- Medium Confidence: Potential concern but context is partially ambiguous → Classify as Level 3 (Review Required) rather than immediately marking Critical.
- Low Confidence: Unclear audio or colloquial phrasing that cannot be verified → Do not fabricate a violation. Mark for manual verification without false alerts.

18. Call Summary Display & Rating Criteria

AI Call Summary Structure:
- Service Type: Booked service (Walking, Grooming, Training, Sitting)
- Summary: Structured 3-part synthesis answering the core questions
- Important Service Information: Timing, pet needs, confirmed commitments
- Issues Detected: Specific categorized breakdown (None, Complaint, Bypass, Payment, Abuse)
- Overall AI Rating & Score:
  🟢 Good (Score >= 8.0 / 10): Service-focused, professional, no policy violations.
  🟡 Review Recommended (Score 5.0 - 7.9 / 10): Complaints, refund requests, sustained unrelated talk.
  🔴 Critical (Score < 5.0 / 10 or Policy Violation): Direct payment, contact sharing, bypass, abuse, threats.

19. Final Acceptance Criteria & Summary of Rules
1. Normal greetings and confirmations are never highlighted.
2. Pet-related information (e.g. "Bruno is not feeling comfortable today") is treated as care context, not a violation.
3. Cancellation and rescheduling are standard service topics, not violations.
4. Discussion of GST and platform charges for clarification is normal and never flagged.
5. Using GST or platform charges to propose off-platform bypass is flagged as Critical.
6. Mobile numbers and WhatsApp transitions for private communication are flagged as Contact Sharing.
7. Any proposal to book or pay outside Petsfolio is flagged as Platform Bypass / Off-Platform Payment.
8. Negative service feedback is treated as a Complaint, not abusive language.
9. Actual insults and threats are flagged as Inappropriate Language / Threatening Communication.
10. Only the offending sentences are highlighted, preserving normal transcript context.
