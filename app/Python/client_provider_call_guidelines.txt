AI Integration – Call Summary & Service Conversation Analysis (Client & Provider Calls)

1. Document Purpose
The purpose of this document is to define the requirements for AI-based Call Summary and Conversation Analysis in the Petsfolio platform.
Petsfolio has introduced Mobile-to-Mobile Calling between the Client/Pet Parent and Service Provider (SP). Calls will be automatically recorded.
The AI system must analyze the recorded conversation and generate a structured Call Summary.
The primary objective is to ensure that conversations between Clients and Service Providers are relevant to the booked Petsfolio service, such as:
- Walking
- Grooming
- Training
- Sitting
The AI must identify and highlight any unrelated, inappropriate, or potentially problematic conversation in the summary.

2. Call Flow
Basic Call Flow:
Client → Petsfolio Mobile-to-Mobile Call → Service Provider
↓
Call Automatically Recorded
↓
AI Processes Call Recording
↓
Speech-to-Text Conversion
↓
Conversation Analysis
↓
Service Relevance Check
↓
AI Call Summary Generated
↓
Unrelated / Important Statements Highlighted
↓
Call Rating / AI Alerts
↓
Admin Can Review

3. AI Analysis Objectives
The AI should analyze the complete conversation and determine:
- What service was discussed?
- Whether the conversation is related to the booked service.
- Whether the Client and SP discussed the service correctly.
- Whether important service information was exchanged.
- Whether any unrelated conversation occurred.
- Whether inappropriate language was used.
- Whether there were complaints or disputes.
- Whether the SP made any unauthorized commitments.
- Whether the Client requested something outside the booked service.
- Whether the call requires Admin attention.

4. Supported Services
The AI must understand the four major Petsfolio service categories.

The AI should recognize conversations related to:
- Walk date
- Walk time
- Walk duration
- Walk location
- Pickup/drop location
- Pet name
- Pet behavior
- Pet walking requirements
- Leash requirements
- Pet safety
- Toilet/poop requirements
- Paw cleaning
- Combing
- Number of walks
- Walk completion
- Delayed walk
- Missed walk
- Walk rescheduling
- Walker arrival
- Walk cancellation
- Walk-related complaints

Example:
Client: "Can you come tomorrow at 7 AM for Bruno's walk?"
SP: "Yes, I will come at 7 AM."
AI Classification: ✅ Service Related – Walking

8. AI Must Understand Service Context
The AI should not only search for specific keywords.
It should understand the context of the conversation.
For example:
"Is 7 okay for tomorrow?"
By itself, this is ambiguous.
But if the previous conversation is:
"Tomorrow's walk is scheduled at 7 AM."
Then the AI should classify:
Service: Walking
Classification: Service Related

9. Unrelated Conversation Detection
The AI must identify conversations that are not related to the Petsfolio service.
Examples include:
- Politics
- Movies
- Personal entertainment
- General gossip
- Personal relationships
- Religion
- Sports
- Unrelated business
- Personal financial discussions
- Other unrelated topics
- Long personal conversations
These statements should be highlighted in the AI Call Summary.

10. Example – Unrelated Conversation
Conversation:
Client: "What time will you come for Bruno's walk?"
SP: "I will come at 7 AM."
Client: "Okay. By the way, did you watch yesterday's cricket match?"
SP: "Yes, it was a very good match."

AI Analysis:
Service-related: ✅ Walking
Unrelated conversation: ⚠️ Cricket discussion
Summary: Client and Service Provider discussed the scheduled walking time. An unrelated discussion regarding a cricket match was also detected.
The unrelated sentence should be highlighted in the Admin Call Summary.

11. Mixed Conversation
A call can contain both service-related and unrelated conversations.
The AI should not mark the entire call as unrelated.
Instead, it should identify individual portions.

Example:
Client: "Can you come at 6 PM for the grooming?"
SP: "Yes, I will be there at 6 PM."
Client: "Okay. I recently bought a new car."
SP: "Oh, which car?"
Client: "It is a Honda."

AI Result:
- Grooming at 6 PM: ✅ Service Related
- New car discussion: ⚠️ Unrelated

12. Inappropriate Language Detection
AI should identify inappropriate or abusive language during the call.
Examples:
- Abusive words
- Offensive language
- Threatening statements
- Harassment
- Personal insults
- Aggressive communication
- Disrespectful communication

The AI should not simply highlight a word because it is potentially ambiguous. It should consider the context.
Example:
Client: "I am very angry because the walker did not arrive."
Classification: ⚠️ Complaint / Negative Sentiment
Client: [Abusive statement directed at SP]
Classification: 🚨 Inappropriate Language

13. Complaint Detection
AI should identify service complaints.
Examples:
- SP arrived late
- SP did not arrive
- Pet was not handled properly
- Grooming was incomplete
- Trainer missed sessions
- Sitting service was not provided properly
- Service quality was poor
- SP behaved improperly
- Client is requesting a refund

Example:
Client: "The walker came 30 minutes late today."
AI Classification:
Service: Walking | Category: Complaint | Priority: Medium

14. Cancellation Detection
AI should identify cancellation-related conversations.
Example:
Client: "I don't need tomorrow's walk. I want to cancel it."
AI Result:
Service: Walking | Topic: Cancellation | Action Required: Review cancellation status/process

15. Rescheduling Detection
Example:
Client: "Can we move today's grooming appointment to tomorrow?"
AI Result:
Service: Grooming | Topic: Rescheduling | Status: Service-related

16. Refund Discussion
AI should identify refund-related discussions.
Example:
Client: "I want my money back because the service was not completed."
AI Result:
🚨 Refund Request Detected
Service: Service identified from booking/call context
The AI should highlight the statement and make it available for Admin review.

17. Outside-Service Request
The Client may ask the SP for something that is not included in the booked service.
Example:
Client: "I booked walking, but can you also groom my dog?"
AI Result:
⚠️ Additional Service Request
Booked Service: Walking | Requested Service: Grooming
This should be highlighted because the request is outside the current service context.

18. Direct Personal Payment Discussion
If a Client and SP discuss making payments outside the Petsfolio platform, the AI should flag the conversation for Admin review.
Example:
Client: "Can I pay you directly instead of using Petsfolio?"
SP: "Yes, you can send it directly."
AI Result:
🚨 Potential Off-Platform Payment Discussion
This should generate an Admin Alert.

19. Contact Sharing Detection
If the conversation includes an attempt to exchange personal contact details outside the platform, AI should flag it according to Petsfolio's configured policy.
Examples:
- Asking for personal phone number
- Sharing another contact number
- WhatsApp contact discussion
- Personal email exchange
AI Result:
⚠️ Potential Contact Sharing Detected

20. Service Quality Analysis
AI should evaluate whether the conversation indicates good or poor service communication.
Possible categories:
- Positive
- Neutral
- Negative
- Complaint
- Escalation Required

21. Call Summary Format
Every completed call should generate a structured summary answering 3 core questions:
1. What was discussed?
2. Was the conversation related to the booked Petsfolio service?
3. Was there anything that requires Admin attention?

22. Overall Call Rating
🟢 Good (Rating >= 8.0 / 10):
- Mostly service-related
- No major complaint
- No policy concern
- Professional communication

🟡 Needs Review / Review Recommended (5.0 - 7.9 / 10):
- Significant unrelated conversation
- Service complaint
- Negative communication
- Potential issue requiring Admin review

🔴 Critical (Rating < 5.0 / 10 or Policy Violation):
- Abusive/threatening communication
- Off-platform payment attempt
- Serious policy violation
- Serious client/SP dispute

23. Conversation Highlighting Categories
🟢 Service Related
🟡 Unrelated Conversation
🔴 Policy Violation / Serious Issue
🟠 Complaint
🔵 Important Service Information
